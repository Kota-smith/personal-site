Blender is where I go to think visually.

What started as curiosity turned into a habit of making things just to see if I could. Product-style renders, looping animations, lighting tests, and small visual ideas that help me understand how form, light, and motion work together.

Not everything here has a purpose beyond learning the tool and trusting the process.

Some experiments land. Some do not. All of them move things forward.
