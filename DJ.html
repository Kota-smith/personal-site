🎧 DJing

Duh.Kota did not plan on becoming a DJ.

He clicked an Instagram ad for YourShot on a whim and figured it would be a learning experience at best. Instead, it turned into six weeks of driving to Los Angeles, showing up to classes, and stepping into a world he had only watched from the outside.

Before that, he had never played for a crowd. Then suddenly he was performing alongside his classmates at Catch One in LA. Real club, real people, real nerves. Hands shaking, heart racing, doing his best to stay present and finish what he started.

It was uncomfortable. It was exciting. It worked.

YourShot took him from practicing in private to showing up in public and proved that you do not need years of experience to take the first real step. You just need the nerve to say yes and see it through.

This was not about having it all figured out. It was about starting. And that part is done.

What comes next is still unfolding.
